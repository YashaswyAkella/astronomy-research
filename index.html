What is beside this quasar?

J1237+3314: is the faint object beside a known quasar physically associated with it?

This archival investigation compares optical and radio positions and examines a spectral feature collected towards the faint component.

The analysis supports a close radio–optical positional association and reports a feature that may be relevant to the system’s interpretation.

Current conclusion: the physical relationship remains unresolved. No confirmed gravitational lens or dual-AGN discovery is claimed. Independent reproduction is pending.

How these projects are presented

The aim is to make the work understandable and inspectable, without making the conclusions stronger than the evidence.

Start with a clear question

Explain what is being investigated and why the available observations might help.

Separate results from interpretation

A measured signal and its possible explanation are different claims. Each needs its own supporting evidence.

Show the methods

Provide code, input references, numerical outputs, and instructions where available.

Keep uncertainty visible

State assumptions, known limitations, alternative explanations, and checks that remain incomplete.

What do the project labels mean?
  • In progress: work is underway and results may change.
  • Exploratory: a documented investigation whose conclusions remain provisional.
  • Archived: work has been paused or completed as a documented project. This does not imply peer review.
  • Peer-reviewed: a linked publication has completed journal peer review. This label applies to that publication, not automatically to every file or later change in its repository.

Sharing code or obtaining an archive DOI does not, by itself, validate a scientific conclusion.

About this website

I am Yashaswy Akella. This website documents my independent astronomy projects using publicly available observations and analysis tools.

My interests include quasars, active galaxies, gravitational lens candidates, and comparing observations across different wavelengths.

This is a personal research portfolio, not the website of an observatory or research institution. Project-specific references, survey credits, software details, and AI-assistance disclosures are provided with each project.
